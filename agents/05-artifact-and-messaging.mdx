# Artifact And Messaging

## Artifact Standard

Never send raw output.

Always include:

- 1 clear action
- 2 missing decisions
- 1 risk

## Share Behavior

Users share the single actionable line.

## Messaging Principles

Do:

- "this is happening"
- "this is missing"
- "do this"

Do not:

- "we built a tool"
- Feature lists
