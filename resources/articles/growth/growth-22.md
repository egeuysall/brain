# SECTION 5: SYSTEMS & TRACKING

## 24. Simple Metrics Dashboard

**Track weekly:**

- New signups
- Activated users (completed core action)
- Weekly active users
- Top acquisition channel
- Churn (if applicable)

**Tools:** Google Sheets, Notion, PostHog (free)

---

## 25. Channel Performance Tracker

**For each channel, track:**

- Time invested
- Users acquired
- Activation rate
- Quality (do they stick around?)

**Double down on what works. Kill what doesn't.**

---

## 26. Weekly Review System

**Every Sunday, answer:**

1. What worked this week?
2. What didn't?
3. Top 3 priorities for next week?
4. Biggest blocker?

**Keep notes. Patterns emerge.**
