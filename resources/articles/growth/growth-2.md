# 🎤 WEBINAR & EVENT FRAMEWORKS

### 42. Webinar Invitation Email

**Context**: Webinars can generate quality leads when done right
**Why Test This**: Live interaction builds trust

**Framework**:

```
Subject: [Workshop] How to [Outcome] in [Timeframe]

Hi [Name],

Hosting a live workshop [Date] at [Time].

**What We're Covering:**
→ [Specific learning]
→ [Specific learning]
→ [Specific learning]

**Why Attend:**
Not a pitch. Working session where you'll leave with [deliverable].

**Who Should Come:**
[Job titles] at [stage] who want [goal]

**Bonus for Attendees:**
- [Resource]
- Recording + slides
- [Extra item]

[CTA: Register] (Limited to [X] people)

Can't attend live? Register for recording.

[Your Name]

P.S. Previous workshop feedback: "[Quote]"
```

**Test Variables:**

- Title: “Webinar” vs. “Workshop” vs. “Masterclass”
- Registration page: Simple vs. detailed
- Limit: Real vs. none
- Reminder sequence: 1 vs. 3 emails

### 43. Webinar Follow-Up Sequence

**Context**: Most value comes from follow-up, not just live event
**Why Test This**: Strike while interest is high

**Framework**:

```
EMAIL 1: Thank You (Within 1 hour)
Subject: Your recording + bonus materials

[Name],

Thanks for joining [workshop topic]!

Here's everything:
→ [Recording]
→ [Slides]
→ [Template mentioned]

BONUS: [Extra resource]

Want to implement this? [Product] automates [specific thing from workshop].

[CTA: Start Free Trial]

Questions? Reply to this.

---

EMAIL 2: Case Study (24 hours)
Subject: How [Company] implemented what we covered

[Name],

Following up on yesterday - real example of this in action.

[Company] implemented [framework] using [Product] and achieved [result] in [time].

Here's how: [Case study link]

Replicate their approach: [CTA]

---

EMAIL 3: Special Offer (48 hours)
Subject: Workshop attendee bonus

[Name],

Because you attended, exclusive offer:

20% off [Product] for 6 months.
Expires [date].
Code: [CODE]

[CTA: Claim Discount]
```

**Test Variables:**

- Email timing: Immediate vs. next day
- Offer size: 10% vs. 20% vs. extended trial
- Sequence length: 2 vs. 3 vs. 4 emails
- Urgency: Time-limited vs. open

### 44. Conference/Event Networking

**Context**: In-person events for relationship building
**Why Test This**: Face-to-face accelerates trust

**Framework**:

```
PRE-EVENT (1 week before):
Subject: Also at [Event]? Let's meet

[Name],

Saw you're attending [Event] - me too!

Grab coffee and discuss [relevant topic]?

I'm free [Day/Time]. Meet at [venue location]?

Looking forward,
[Your Name]

---

AT EVENT:
Send message: "Hey! I'm at [booth/area]. Come say hi 👋"

---

POST-EVENT (Same evening):
Subject: Great meeting you today!

[Name],

Enjoyed our chat about [specific topic].

As promised: [resource/intro mentioned]

Let's continue - [next step suggestion]

[CTA or calendar link]

[Your Name]

[Include photo if you took one]
```

**Test Variables:**

- Pre-event timing: 1 week vs. 2 days
- Meeting location: Specific vs. flexible
- Post-event timing: Same day vs. next day
- Follow-up depth: Brief vs. detailed
