# 🎁 LEAD MAGNET FRAMEWORKS

### 28. High-Converting Lead Magnet Titles

**Context**: Title affects opt-in rate significantly
**Why Test This**: Better title = more leads

**Title Formulas**:

```
1. "[Number] Ways to [Outcome] Without [Pain Point]"
2. "The [Time]-Minute [Process] to [Result]"
3. "How [Company] [Achieved Result] Using [Method]"
4. "[Type] Guide to [Goal] for [ICP]"
5. "The [Asset] That Helped Us [Result]"

Landing Page Structure:
Headline: [Title formula]
Subhead: "Join [number] [ICP] who've [benefit]"
Bullets: 3-5 specific learnings
Form: Email only (or +1 field maximum)
CTA: "Send Me The [Asset Name]"
```

**Test Variables:**

- Title format: How-to vs. list vs. toolkit
- Specificity: General vs. niche
- Form fields: Email only vs. +name vs. +company
- Delivery: Immediate vs. email course format
