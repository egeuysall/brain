# SECTION 3: GROWTH CHANNELS

## BUILD IN PUBLIC

### 9. Daily Content System (15 min/day)

**Monday:** Metrics update

**Tuesday:** Problem/solution post

**Wednesday:** Behind-the-scenes progress

**Thursday:** User testimonial/win

**Friday:** Lesson learned

**Format:**

```
Short hook (1 line)
Main content (3-5 lines)
Visual (screenshot/GIF)
Question to audience

```

---

### 10. Engagement Pipeline (30 min/day)

**Step 1:** Search Twitter/Threads for:

- "[problem you solve]"
- "[competitor] alternative"
- "frustrated with [thing]"

**Step 2:** Reply helpfully to 20 posts:

```
[Helpful tip related to their problem]

BTW, building something that might help: [Product] - [one line]

Happy to give you early access if useful.

```

**Expected:** 1-2 signups/day

**Reality variance:** More realistic = 1-2 signups per WEEK when starting. Unknown accounts get suppressed by algorithms. Takes 2-3 weeks of daily engagement to build visibility. Don't give up after week 1.

## COLD OUTREACH

### 11. Value-First Cold Email (100 emails/week)

**Template:**

```
Subject: Quick idea for [Company]

Hi [Name],

Noticed [specific observation about them].

Most [their role] struggle with [specific problem].

Built [Product] to solve this. Would you try it?

Free forever + 15-min setup call if helpful.

[Your name]

```

**Tools:** Hunter.io (free: 50 emails/month), regular Gmail

**Expected:** 100 emails = 2-4 signups

**Reality variance:** First 100 emails often get 0-1 signups as you learn what messaging works. By email 300-500, you'll dial it in to 2-4 per 100. Also requires 2-3 hours of research time per 100 emails, not just 30 minutes of sending.

---

### 12. LinkedIn DM Sequence

**Step 1:** Connect with 20 people/day in your ICP

**Step 2:** After acceptance, send value (not pitch)

**Step 3:** Soft intro to product

**DM Template:**

```
Hey [Name], thanks for connecting!

Saw your post about [topic].

Quick question - how are you currently handling [problem]?

[If they respond]

Interesting! I'm building [Product] for exactly that. Would love your take on it if you're open to trying: [link]

```

---

### 13. Micro-Influencer Outreach (20 people)

**Find:** 500-5K follower accounts in your niche

**Engage first:** Like/comment for 3-5 days

**DM:**

```
Hey [Name],

Love your content on [topic].

Building [Product] for [ICP]. Would you try it and share honest feedback?

Happy to give you:
- Lifetime free access
- Custom feature for your workflow
- Discount code for your audience

No pressure if not a fit.

[Link]

```

**Expected:** 20 messages = 1 will share = 10-50 signups

**Reality variance:** Most micro-influencers won't respond (they get 100s of these requests). Realistic = 20 messages → 3-5 responses → 0-1 shares → 5-15 signups if lucky. But one good influencer can send 50+ users, so still worth trying.

## COMMUNITY GROWTH

### 14. The Strategic Launch Stack

**One launch, multiple platforms:**

**Day 1:** ProductHunt

**Day 2:** Hacker News

**Day 3:** Reddit (r/SideProject, r/alphaandbetausers)

**Day 4-7:** Indie Hackers, Dev.to, niche communities

**Use same story, different angles for each platform.**

**Expected:** 200-500 total signups

**Reality variance:** Coordinated launches are exhausting and results vary wildly. Realistic = 50-200 signups total, with 20-50% activation rate. One platform might pop off while others flop. You won't know which until you try.

---

### 15. Partnership Outreach (10 complementary tools)

**Who to target:** Similar-stage tools your ICP already uses

**Pitch Template:**

```
Subject: Partnership idea - [Your Product] + [Their Product]

Hey [Founder],

We both serve [same ICP]. [X]% of my users also use [Their Product].

Partnership idea:
- Recommend each other
- Simple integration (or Zapier)
- Co-create content

Value for you: Access to my [X] users
Value for me: Credibility + your users

15-min call to explore?

```

**Expected:** 10 pitches = 1-2 partnerships = 20-50 users each

**Reality variance:** Partnerships take 2-3 months to materialize. Most pitches get ghosted. Realistic = 10 pitches → 2-3 calls → 0-1 actual partnership → 10-30 users over 3 months. Long-term play, not quick wins.

## CONTENT TACTICS

### 16. The "Ultimate Guide" Play

**Create one valuable resource:**

- "Ultimate Guide to [Problem]"
- "[Number] Templates for [Task]"
- Free tool/calculator
- Comparison guide

**Promote:** Twitter thread, LinkedIn post, Reddit (if allowed), communities

**Link naturally to product throughout.**

**Expected:** 100-500 views/month = 5-25 signups/month

**Reality variance:** First content pieces get 10-50 views, not 100-500. Takes 3-6 months of consistent content to build SEO traffic. Month 1 might only bring 1-2 signups. Month 6+ is where this pays off.

---

### 17. Comparison Content (SEO play)

**Create pages:**

- "[Competitor] vs [Your Product]"
- "[Competitor] Alternative"
- "[Category] Tools Compared"

**Be honest about both.** Explain who should use what.

**Long-term traffic source.**
