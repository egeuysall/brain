# 🎨 VIDEO & CREATIVE FRAMEWORKS

### 34. 60-Second Explainer Script

**Context**: Short videos for attention-limited platforms
**Why Test This**: Video typically outperforms static content

**Framework**:

```
[0-5 sec]: Problem Hook
"Still wasting 10 hours/week on [manual task]?"

[6-15 sec]: Agitate
"Most [ICP] struggle with [challenge]. That costs [impact]."

[16-35 sec]: Solution Demo
"[Product] automates this in 3 steps:
1. [Action] - show it
2. [Action] - show it
3. [Action] - show it"

[36-50 sec]: Social Proof
"[Company] went from [before] to [after] in [timeframe]."

[51-60 sec]: CTA
"Start free at [URL]. No credit card needed."
```

**Test Variables:**

- Hook type: Question vs. stat vs. story
- Demo style: Screen recording vs. animation vs. live action
- Proof format: Testimonial vs. stat vs. logo
- CTA: Trial vs. demo vs. resource

### 35. Product Hunt Launch Copy

**Context**: Product Hunt can drive significant early users
**Why Test This**: Good launch = visibility + backlinks + users

**Framework**:

```
Tagline (60 chars max):
[Action Verb] [Outcome] in [Time/Manner]

Short Description (260 chars max):
"[Product] helps [ICP] [achieve outcome] by [differentiator]."

First Comment Template:
"Hey Product Hunt! 👋

We're launching [Product] today after [time] building.

**The Problem:**
[Personal story of pain point - 2-3 sentences]

**Our Solution:**
[Product] [core function] so you can [outcome].

**What Makes Us Different:**
→ [Differentiator 1]
→ [Differentiator 2]
→ [Differentiator 3]

**Special for PH:**
Use code PRODUCTHUNT for [offer]

Would love your feedback on [specific question]!

[Founder Name(s)]"
```

**Test Variables:**

- Launch day: Tuesday vs. Wednesday
- Tagline style: Benefit vs. feature
- Special offer: Discount vs. lifetime deal vs. extended trial
- Founder story: Include vs. omit
