# SECTION 4: SCALE & OPTIMIZE

## CONVERSION OPTIMIZATION

### 18. Founder-Led Demos (High-touch onboarding)

**Who to offer:** Signups who don't activate in 48 hours

**DM:**

```
Hey [Name], saw you signed up but haven't [core action] yet.

Want a quick 10-min call? I'll show you how to get value fast + set it up for your workflow.

Not a sales call - just want you to get value.

[Calendar link]

```

**Demo script:**

1. Learn about them (2 min)
2. Show ONLY relevant features (5 min)
3. Help them complete first action (3 min)

**Expected:** 10 demos = 7-8 activations

**Reality variance:** Assumes people show up (50% no-show rate is common). Assumes your demo is good (takes practice). Realistic first 10 demos = 3-5 completed, 2-3 activations. Gets better with practice.

---

### 19. User Interview Loop

**Interview active users:**

**Questions:**

- What made you try this?
- How do you use it?
- What value are you getting?
- What's frustrating?
- Would you pay? How much?

**Ask for:**

- Testimonial quote
- Feature feedback
- Referrals

**Expected:** 10 interviews = 7 testimonials + 2-3 referrals

**Reality variance:** Users ghost interview requests (30-40% no-show). Some give mediocre testimonials. Realistic = 10 requests → 6 completed → 4 good testimonials → 0-2 referrals. Still worth it for product insights alone.

---

### 20. Testimonial Collection System

**When to ask:** After user gets a win

**Email:**

```
Hey [Name]!

Saw you [achieved result with product]. Awesome!

Quick favor - mind if I use this as a testimonial?

"[Quote about their result]"

I'll send for approval before posting.

Thanks!

```

**Use testimonials:** Landing page, social posts, outreach

## RETENTION & REFERRALS

### 21. The Welcome Email Sequence

**Email 1 (Immediate):**

```
Subject: Welcome to [Product]! Start here 👇

Hi [Name],

Your account is ready. Do this in 5 minutes:

1. [First action]
2. [Second action]
3. [Get first win]

Stuck? Just reply - I read everything.

[Your Name]

```

**Email 2 (Day 2):**

```
Subject: Did you get your first [result]?

Quick check-in - have you [core action] yet?

This is what separates users who love [Product] from those who don't get value.

[Link to feature] - takes 2 mins

Need help? Reply and I'll hop on a call.

```

**Email 3 (Day 7):**

```
Subject: How's [Product] working?

Hey [Name],

You're 1 week in. How's it going?

What's working? What's confusing?

Hit reply - I respond to everyone.

```

### 22. Simple Referral Program

**Add to product after user success:**

```
"Loving [Product]?

```

**Incentives:**

- Free month/credits
- Premium feature
- Lifetime discount

### 23. Re-engagement Email (Inactive users)

**Send after 7 days inactive:**

```
Subject: Everything ok with [Product]?

Hey [Name],

Noticed you haven't been active.

Did you:
→ Run into issues? [Support link]
→ Find what you needed?
→ Need a refresher? [Quick start guide]

Reply and let me know how I can help.

Or if it's not a fit, no hard feelings - would love to know why so I can improve.

```
