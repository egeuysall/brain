# SECTION 2: QUICK WINS

## 4. Friends & Family Launch

**DM Script:**

```
Hey! I finally launched [Product].

It's [one sentence what it does].

Would you try it and give me brutally honest feedback?

[Link]

```

**Who to ask:** Former colleagues, online friends, Twitter mutuals, anyone who has the problem.

**Goal:** 5-10 users in 24 hours

**Reality variance:** Might only get 2-3 if your network is small or they're uncomfortable supporting early-stage products. That's okay - keep going.

## 5. ProductHunt Launch

**Launch Checklist:**

- Engage on PH for 2 weeks before (comment, upvote daily)
- Post at 12:01am PST on Tuesday/Wednesday
- Reply to EVERY comment in first 3 hours
- Share to Twitter/Threads throughout day

**First Comment Template:**

```
Hey PH! 👋

Built [Product] because [personal problem in 1 sentence].

What it does: [3 bullets]
Why different: [1 unique thing]
Special for PH: [Discount code]

Feedback welcome!

```

**Realistic results:** Top 10 = 20-80 signups

**Reality variance:** In 2025, ProductHunt is saturated. Top 10 might get 10-30 *actual activated users*. Top 20 might only get 5-15. Some launches flop completely despite good products. Don't put all eggs in this basket.

**Submit to these 10 first:**

## 7. Reddit Soft Launch

**Find 3-5 relevant subreddits:** r/SaaS, r/startups, r/[yourniche]

**Contribute first:** Comment helpfully for 1 week, build karma

**Launch Post Template:**

```
Title: "Built [tool] to solve [problem] - feedback wanted"

[2-sentence personal story why you built this]

**The Problem:** [What you saw]
**What I Built:** [Product] - [what it does]
**Features:** [3 bullets]

Giving free lifetime access to first [X] who try it and give feedback: [link]

Not pitching - genuinely want to know if this is useful.

```

**Expected:** 10-30 signups per good post

**Reality variance:** Many subreddits will remove self-promotion. New accounts get shadowbanned. Hostile comments are common. Realistic = 0-15 signups per post, and some posts get deleted immediately. Test 3-5 subreddits to find which ones work.
