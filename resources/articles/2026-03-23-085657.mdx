Each year, a16z partners publish their “Big Ideas” for the next 12 months, calling shots on where tech founders will take us next.

Our friends across the firm have already published a three-part series featuring Big Ideas from partners on **[Bio + Health](https://www.a16z.news/p/big-ideas-2026-part-1)**, **[American Dynamism](https://www.a16z.news/p/big-ideas-2026-part-2)**, and **[Crypto](https://www.a16z.news/p/big-ideas-2026-part-3)**.

Today, 14 investors and operating partners on the a16z speedrun team weigh in with their own Big Ideas for 2026.

_By Jonathan Lai_-**[X](https://x.com/Tocelot/status/1998791674353656257)** | **[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7404557410863837185/)**

> _World models will also likely give rise to not just a single game, but an entire new category of generative world experiences. You could have a horror experience where you’re hiding from generated monsters, or a D&D experience where you’re roaming an infinite fantasy world with friends._

_By Troy Kirwin_-**[X](https://x.com/tkexpress11/status/1998852154510217325)** | **[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7404606634611814402/)**

> _While the west coast, Patagonia-wearing VCs and the east coast, PE suits used to live in different universes… In 2026 with AI, I believe, those worlds converge._

_By Fareed Mosavat_-**[X](https://x.com/far33d/status/1999173392394781170)** | **[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7404939499656527875/)**

> _Right now, most AI tools are built for one human + one model in a private workspace. ChatGPT, Cursor, Claude. Incredibly powerful, but currently optimized for individuals. The impact is massive: drafts, code, specs, campaigns, workflows. But almost none of it is shared, aligned, or contextualized across a team._

_By Macy Mills_-**[X](https://x.com/_CallMeMacy/status/1999161492093198388)** | **[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7404926158376570880/)**

> _Say you need 50 qualified sales meetings. Instead of buying another AI tool, you post a bounty: “$500 per meeting booked.” AI agents compete. Whoever performs best gets paid. We already do this with bug bounties, Kaggle, hackathons. Why not for AI agents going after real business outcomes?_

_By Emily Bennett_-**[X](https://x.com/emilybenn12/status/1999531989469397437)** | **[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7405301940743700481/)**

> _Picture an institution where courses, advising, research collaboration, and even building operations continuously adapt based on data feedback loops. Schedules optimize themselves. Reading lists evolve nightly and rewrite themselves as new research appears. Learning paths shift in real time to meet each student’s pace and context._

_By Josh Lu_-**[X](https://x.com/JoshLu/status/1999506541058396248)** | **[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7405272477012103168/)**

> _For over two centuries, our world has been shaped by one idea: economies of scale. This mindset delivered extraordinary progress: affordable/accessible nutrition, reliable housing, global transportation (and the accompanying shrinking of supply lines and cultural divides), and more. But in 2026, I believe this paradigm flips._

_By Tom Hammer_-**[X](https://x.com/tmhammer/status/1999900180200804720?s=20)** | **[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/tom-hammer_my-big-idea-for-2026-the-hacker-house-activity-7405666080716873728-mZKN?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAANhXd4BubFsuhjEDnu5ZDJOLMRTN4dE3X4)**

> _Ambition breeds ambition, and society needs ways to get the smartest and hungriest young people in a single room together. Residencies are one of the cheapest and most effective ways to create this clustering / cohesion—the expansion of solo founding / building only heightens the need for cohesive communities_

_By Ryan Rigney_-**[X](https://x.com/RKRigney/status/1999871027296522521?s=20) | [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ryanrigney_my-big-idea-for-2026-is-that-we-will-see-activity-7405635871045070848-85e0?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAANhXd4BubFsuhjEDnu5ZDJOLMRTN4dE3X4)**

> _The word on the street is that the kids don’t read anymore. Everybody overdosed on YouTube Shorts and Bang energy drinks and forgot how to flip a page. So the story goes. But something strange is starting to happen. Startups are talking about wanting to hire “storytellers.”_

_By Lester Chen_-**[X](https://x.com/Chen/status/2000642704225788403?s=20) | [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/lester-chen_in-2026-curation-will-be-king-while-everyone-activity-7406409061400973312-wQOF?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAANhXd4BubFsuhjEDnu5ZDJOLMRTN4dE3X4)**

> _The best movie/game/book recos I’ve gotten came from word of mouth or short-form videos, which led me to YouTube, which I then consumed while then going to Reddit to discuss. As cliche as it sounds, can AI solve this? An ‘agent of my tastes’ recording the things i consume and suggesting the most relevant pieces of media worth spending my time on._

_By Sam Shank_-**[X](https://x.com/samshank/status/2000630854901481533?s=20) | [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/samshank_shopping-online-still-feels-like-a-chore-activity-7406395817152008193-0bDH?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAANhXd4BubFsuhjEDnu5ZDJOLMRTN4dE3X4)**

> _This past year, I’ve had a front-row seat watching teams at a16z speedrun build AI agent companies across legal, logistics, manufacturing, and other complex, operational domains. What’s striking isn’t just how capable these agents are, it’s that they already take responsibility for outcomes, not just information. Seeing that up close has changed how I think about e-commerce._
>
> _I’ve spent much of my career around marketplaces and the classic playbook was powerful: bring fragmented supply online, aggregate it in one place, make it easy to search, and reduce friction at checkout._

_By Andrew Lee_-**[X](https://x.com/ndrewlee/status/2001011358360568072?s=20) | [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ndrewlee_the-slower-an-industry-adopts-new-technology-activity-7407180611309912064-9-c0?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAANhXd4BubFsuhjEDnu5ZDJOLMRTN4dE3X4)**

> _Back in 2010, Ben Horowitz said some startups should go fat—build the full stack, own distribution, and move fast—while Fred Wilson preached lean. Fifteen years later, the debate’s settled: in the AI era, fat startups win. A fat startup ships outcomes, not features. It bundles software, data, hardware, and human ops into one integrated product that actually gets the job done._

_By Marcus Segal_-**[X](https://x.com/marcussegal/status/2001408643753984327?s=20) | [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/marcussegal_most-of-ais-impact-so-far-has-been-efficiency-based-activity-7407170581265174529-P--U?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAANhXd4BubFsuhjEDnu5ZDJOLMRTN4dE3X4)**

> _Predictive health could transform the health insurance industry from a cost of treatment model to a cost of prevention model. Smart cities powered by AI that integrates traffic, utilities, school and safety data to create better urban environments. Natural disaster prediction and remediation planning to save lives and trillions of dollars._

_By Lejla Johnsen_-**[X](https://x.com/custo_lejla) | [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lejla-custo/)**

> _AI is doing the grunt work so we can finally master human work. This speed of change is pushing us toward what truly matters: self mastery. We can’t control the external, but we can strengthen our internal operating systems by using tools that scale emotional intelligence, foster faith, and bridge the isolation AI risks creating._

_By Kenan Saleh_-**[X](https://x.com/kenanhsaleh/status/2001683778771530228?s=20) | [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kenansaleh_in-2026-i-believe-well-see-a-consumer-ai-activity-7407450360262316032-o-Gq?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAANhXd4BubFsuhjEDnu5ZDJOLMRTN4dE3X4)**

> _I’m especially excited about products that use AI to make previously expensive services cheaper and more accessible, sometimes using human-in-the-loop to start. This includes things like AI travel agents, personal assistants, matchmakers, therapists, tutors, and more._

_That’s it for our roundup of the a16z speedrun team’s Big Ideas for 2026. Have thoughts you’d like to share? Hit us in the comments below._
