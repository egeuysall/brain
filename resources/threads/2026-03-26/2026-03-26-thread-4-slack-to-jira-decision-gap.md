## Post Text (Key Excerpt)

```text
Decisions get made fast in Slack because that's where everyone is.
But what happens to that context afterwards?
Does it make it into Jira reliably, or is there always some gap?
```

## Why It Matches Ryva ICP

This is the exact GitHub/Slack/Jira mismatch pain: decisions happen in chat, but system-of-record artifacts drift. It highlights hidden decision context and recurring accountability gaps.

## Underlying Problem

Teams make execution decisions in chat but fail to persist rationale and ownership in tracked work.

## Suggested Public Response (Copy)

```text
When Slack becomes the decision layer and Jira stays the task layer, teams pay a “context tax” every sprint. The fix is usually enforcing one lightweight rule: no final decision is valid until owner + rationale is attached to the work item.
```

## Suggested DM Idea (Copy)

