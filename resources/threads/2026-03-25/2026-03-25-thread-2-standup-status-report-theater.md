## Snapshot

- Role signal: Experienced SWE across multiple companies
- Situation type: Ritualized status reporting disguised as unblock sync
- Tool/context signal: Blockers handled later in chat, not during standup

## Pain Summary

After nine companies, the author sees the same anti-pattern: standups are management-facing performance updates, while real problem-solving happens elsewhere.

## Why This Is High-Signal

- Repeated pain across many teams, not a one-off complaint.
- Clear distinction between ceremony and real coordination flow.
- Strong “meeting does not represent project state” signal.

## Suggested Public Reply (Copy)

```text
You’re describing a common split between visible process and real execution: standup is where people report, while actual unblocking happens in side channels. That usually means the team lacks a shared project-state layer, so meetings become theater instead of coordination.
```
