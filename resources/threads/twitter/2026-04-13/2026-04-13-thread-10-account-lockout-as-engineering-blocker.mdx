## Platform

## Link

## Original Post (Key Excerpt)

```text
A paid engineering tool account flagged without clear resolution path can hard-block delivery and support workflows.
```

## Why It Matches Ryva ICP

Clear delivery risk signal: external platform dependency, blocked execution, and weak escalation visibility.

## Underlying Problem

When a core tooling account is locked, teams lose shipping capacity without a reliable ownership and escalation chain.

## Suggested Public Response (Copy)

```text
This is a real ops risk. Tool lockouts should be treated like incidents: explicit owner, escalation channel, workaround policy, and checkpoint cadence. Otherwise one account issue silently blocks delivery.
```

## Suggested DM Idea (Copy)

```text
Do you have a fallback path for tool-account lockouts, or does delivery pause until support resolves it?
```
