## Why It Matches Ryva ICP

## Underlying Problem

Project state is not visible by default, so teams pay recurring meeting overhead to reconstruct it.

## Suggested Public Response (Copy)

```text
Accurate. A tracker becomes noise when it records fields but not intent and ownership. Teams move faster when every ticket captures: owner, latest decision, current blocker, and link to the PR/discussion where the decision changed.
```
