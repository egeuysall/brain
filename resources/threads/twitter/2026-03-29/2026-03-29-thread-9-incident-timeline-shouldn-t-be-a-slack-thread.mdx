## Post Text (Key Excerpt)

```text
Your incident timeline shouldn't be a Slack thread you scroll through at 3am.  OneUptime auto-generates incident timelines — every alert, every status change, every responder action. One source of truth, not a forensic dig through chat history.
```

## Why It Matches Ryva ICP

It captures incident context scattered in Slack and the cost of reconstructing state during high-pressure work.

## Underlying Problem

Critical decisions are implicit in chat, so requirement changes propagate inconsistently.

## Suggested Public Response (Copy)

```text
Exactly. Incident response slows down when timeline context is reconstructed from chat. The highest leverage change is one shared incident thread with owner, latest hypothesis, and causal PR/service updates in real time.
```

## Suggested DM Idea (Copy)

```text
When a requirement changes mid-sprint, who is explicitly responsible for recording the final decision and downstream owner?
```
