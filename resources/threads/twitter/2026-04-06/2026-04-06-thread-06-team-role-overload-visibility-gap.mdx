## Original Post (Key Excerpt)

```text
List of professionals in this role:
Structural Engineer
Project Manager
Project Planner
Geotechnical Engineer
Site Engineer
QA/QC Intern
All these to be done by only one person...
```

## Why It Matches Ryva ICP

Even outside software, this is a clean overload signal: one person is absorbing planning, execution, and quality responsibilities that should be visible and separable.

## Underlying Problem

When one operator holds too many responsibilities, bottlenecks stay hidden until deadlines slip or quality drops.

## Suggested Public Response (Copy)

```text
This is what invisible workload looks like. Once planning, execution, and QA collapse into one person, the team loses any honest view of risk. Work appears assigned, but capacity and ownership are already broken underneath it.
```
