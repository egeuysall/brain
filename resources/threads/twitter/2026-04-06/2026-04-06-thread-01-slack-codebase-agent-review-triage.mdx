## Original Post (Key Excerpt)

```text
If I was on an engineering team, I'd hook this up to my codebase and drop it in Slack.

It explains code, reviews PRs, triages issues, automatically, on a schedule, day and night.

Incredible alpha in coding-adjacent agents that live where people are already working.
```

## Why It Matches Ryva ICP

This is a direct signal that teams want review and triage context embedded in the repo plus Slack instead of split across tools and meetings.

## Underlying Problem

Review state, issue state, and code context live in different places, so teams rebuild understanding every time work moves.

## Suggested Public Response (Copy)

```text
The strongest part is “live where people are already working.” Most teams do not need another dashboard. They need repo context, review state, and issue ownership to stay attached to the same work item when it moves through Slack and GitHub.
```

## Suggested DM Idea (Copy)

