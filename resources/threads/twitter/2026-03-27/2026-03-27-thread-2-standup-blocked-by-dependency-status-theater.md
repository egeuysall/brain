## Post Text (Key Excerpt)

```text
Monday standup. 14 people in a room.
The senior dev says "I'm blocked by a dependency." ...
"let's deprioritize the blocker and re-scope." Everyone nods.
... We do this every week.
```

## Why It Matches Ryva ICP

It shows a classic standup ritual where blockers are named but not operationally resolved. The team performs alignment without explicit ownership or next actions.

## Underlying Problem

Blockers are discussed socially, not converted into accountable, trackable execution decisions.

## Suggested Public Response (Copy)

```text
This is the hidden cost of “healthy-looking” standups: blocker language without ownership language. If no one leaves with a named owner, decision, and deadline, the meeting is status theater.
```

## Suggested DM Idea (Copy)

```text
Your example is painfully common: everyone nods, nobody owns the unblock plan. Want a one-question standup rule that forces clarity without adding more meeting time?
```
