## Why It Matches Ryva ICP

It describes process pressure measured as compliance/attendance instead of outcome quality, a strong signal of status-theater risk in engineering orgs.

## Underlying Problem

Teams are being managed by usage metrics rather than delivery and decision quality.

## Suggested Public Response (Copy)

```text
This distinction matters: adoption metrics are not execution metrics. When teams are graded on tool usage instead of decision quality and shipped outcomes, you create performative process. That is usually when trust drops and real blockers stop surfacing early.
```
