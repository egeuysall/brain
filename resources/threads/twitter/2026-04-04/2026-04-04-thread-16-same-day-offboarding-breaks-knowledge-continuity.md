## Why It Matches Ryva ICP

The post exposes abrupt offboarding and standup exclusion, a direct continuity and handoff risk in active engineering teams.

## Underlying Problem

Sudden exits can leave undocumented work ownership and decision context behind.

## Suggested Public Response (Copy)

```text
This is exactly where silent risk enters the system. Same-day exits without handoff leave hidden dependencies that surface weeks later. A lightweight exit checklist (active tasks, open decisions, primary owners, rollback notes) prevents that continuity gap.
```

## Suggested DM Idea (Copy)

```text
When someone exits abruptly, do you capture their in-flight decisions in one recoverable place?
```
