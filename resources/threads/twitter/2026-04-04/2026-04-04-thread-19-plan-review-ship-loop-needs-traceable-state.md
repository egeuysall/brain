## Underlying Problem

Fast loops can work well but may become non-transferable if key decisions stay implicit.

## Suggested Public Response (Copy)

```text
Tight loops win when the state is explicit. “Plan → review → ship” scales if each step leaves traceable context: intent, owner, and outcome. Otherwise the loop works for one operator but breaks when teammates or on-call need to reconstruct why decisions were made.
```
