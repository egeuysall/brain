## Why It Matches Ryva ICP

This maps to leadership-level execution design: shifting from task completion to outcome ownership and explicit accountability in team operations.

## Underlying Problem

Task-driven execution obscures accountability and weakens outcome alignment across functions.

## Suggested Public Response (Copy)

```text
The KPI-over-task ownership point is important. Teams usually stall when work is assigned as activity instead of accountable outcomes. Making ownership measurable at the outcome level is often what clears decision ambiguity in delivery.
```
