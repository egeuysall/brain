```text
The hardest part of running a SaaS isn't the code. It's context switching. Afternoon: fix a bug. Evening: answer support emails. Night: write a blog post. ## Why It Matches Ryva ICP
```

This is founder-operator ICP pain: one person handles bugs, support, growth, docs, and review, causing heavy context-switch tax.

## Underlying Problem

Critical responsibilities are spread across domains with no protected focus window or coordination layer.

## Suggested Public Response (Copy)

