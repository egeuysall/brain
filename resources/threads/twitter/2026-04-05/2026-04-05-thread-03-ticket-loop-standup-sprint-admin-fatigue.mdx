## Post Text (Key Excerpt)

```text
software development gets boring when it becomes just a job. tickets in. tickets out. same standup. same sprint. same codebase. after a while, it stops feeling like engineering and starts feeling like admin. the boring part isn’t writing code.
```

## Why It Matches Ryva ICP

This captures small-team execution fatigue: repetitive ticket churn, recurring standups, and sprint rituals replacing meaningful engineering outcomes.

## Underlying Problem

Process overhead dominates the week while decision quality and delivery learning stay flat.

## Suggested Public Response (Copy)

```text
That “tickets in, tickets out” loop is a real warning sign. When standup and sprint rituals repeat without sharper decisions, engineering feels like admin. A lightweight fix is forcing each cycle to produce one explicit decision and one owner-visible risk.
```

## Suggested DM Idea (Copy)

```text
In your current sprint cycle, what artifact actually changes decisions, not just status reporting?
```
