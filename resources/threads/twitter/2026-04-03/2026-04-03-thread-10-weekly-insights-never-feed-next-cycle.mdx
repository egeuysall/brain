## Post Text (Key Excerpt)

```text
The first failure mode: Claude captures observations but never revisits them. Insights from last week don't inform this week's work. There's no mechanism to test whether a pattern holds, promote it when it does, or discard it when it doesn't. https://t.co/1SkgReYeLH
```

## Why It Matches Ryva ICP

It points to lost decision continuity across weeks, where observations are captured but not operationalized, causing repeated mistakes and context loss.

## Underlying Problem

Teams are collecting signals without a mechanism to convert them into persistent operating decisions.

## Suggested Public Response (Copy)

