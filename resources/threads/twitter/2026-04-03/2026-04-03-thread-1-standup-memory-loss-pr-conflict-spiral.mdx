```text
A normal friday in Bangalore as a software engineer: 1. The standup ends at 11:10. But nobody remembers what they agreed. 2. A small change turns into 6 files, 2 migrations and some PR conflicts! 3. By 4pm you’re panicking and doing merges and saying “will monitor”. 4. 6pm:…
```

## Why It Matches Ryva ICP

It describes a concrete day-level failure pattern: standup commitments are forgotten, a small change balloons into PR conflicts, and the team ends in reactive merge-and-monitor mode.

## Underlying Problem

Execution context is not preserved after syncs, so teams rediscover decisions under deadline pressure.

## Suggested Public Response (Copy)

```text
This is exactly the cost of “spoken coordination.” If agreements from standup are not written with owner + next checkpoint, a small change becomes PR churn by afternoon. The issue is not effort, it is missing state continuity.
```

## Suggested DM Idea (Copy)

```text
After standup, where does your team record owner + next checkpoint so people do not relitigate decisions later?
```
