## Why It Matches Ryva ICP

It gives a specific failure pattern: long-lived PRs, weak approval language, and loss of testing rationale.

## Underlying Problem

Delayed PR cycles erase decision context, so risky changes merge without explicit test intent.

## Suggested Public Response (Copy)

```text
The two-week PR is the smell. Once review intent collapses to "looks good," nobody can reconstruct what risk was accepted. Teams need explicit decision notes before merge, not after.
```

