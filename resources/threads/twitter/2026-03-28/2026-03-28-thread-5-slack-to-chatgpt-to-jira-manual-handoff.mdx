## Why It Matches Ryva ICP

This is a concrete, messy workflow where decisions are scattered and repeatedly reformatted before landing in tickets. It is a direct signal of implicit decisions and brittle ownership transfer.

## Underlying Problem

Critical context gets transformed across tools, so meaning and ownership degrade at each step.
