```text
The magic formula nobody talks about: high-bandwidth sync + low-interruption async. Open plan offices kill the second part completely. You cant do deep work when someone is having a standup 3 feet from your desk.
```

## Why It Matches Ryva ICP

It calls out the delivery cost of frequent standup interruptions and poor async structure. This is consistent with teams where meetings substitute for reliable project-state visibility.

## Underlying Problem

Teams lose execution capacity when standup-driven sync interrupts deep work without improving shared project clarity.

## Suggested Public Response (Copy)

```text
Yes. High-frequency sync without durable async context just fragments focus. The fix is fewer interruption points and better written state so meetings validate progress instead of reconstructing it.
```
